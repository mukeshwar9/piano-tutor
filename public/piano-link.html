In tempo mode the metronome clicks every beat and the count-in clicks one bar before every start. With a loop running and the ramp on, each clean pass raises the speed by 5% up to the target and a mistake holds it.

The bulb follows what you play: slow colours, from the palette you choose, that blend chords and fade to a warm glow when you stop. Turning it off puts the bulb back as it was.
